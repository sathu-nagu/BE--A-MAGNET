02 / ENERGY

Positive Energy

Explore practices that help you focus on hope, encouragement and what uplifts you.

A THOUGHT TO EXPLORE

Choose what helps you move forward.

Positive energy is not about pretending every day is perfect. It is about noticing what supports you and making room for helpful thoughts, actions and connections.

SIMPLE PRACTICES

Start with small steps

  • Begin the day with one encouraging thought.
  • Spend a little time on an activity that restores you.
  • Notice the people and environments that help you feel supported.
YOUR ONE-MINUTE PRACTICE

Take a moment for yourself

Write down one thing you are looking forward to, then choose one small action that moves you towards it.
